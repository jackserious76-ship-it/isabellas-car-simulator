# Isabella's Car Simulator

A 3D driving game that runs in the browser. One HTML file, no install.

- 65 cars, 100 boats, 100 hotels, 100 businesses
- A big beach city, an ocean with treasure islands, drifting, flying and stunt spins
- A roulette casino (play money only)
- Your coins save in your browser, even if you close the tab

## Play

Save `index.html` and open it in Chrome, Safari or Edge (needs internet for the 3D engine and fonts).
